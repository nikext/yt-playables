# Ramen Rush

*A night-market noodle stall where every second counts.*

You run a tiny ramen stall under paper lanterns. Customers walk up to the counter with an
order bubble made only of icons: a broth, a noodle, one to three toppings and, later, a
drink. Start the noodles boiling, ladle the broth while they cook, add the toppings, drain
the noodles into the bowl and slide it across the counter before their patience runs out.
Keep two, three or four bowls going at once. Fast, correct bowls earn coins, tips and a
streak bonus. A wrong bowl or a customer who gives up costs reputation. Each night ends
with a star rating against the day's coin goal and an upgrade shop. Twenty nights make the
campaign, and the market stays open after that with endless, ever busier nights.

## How to play

- **Noodles**: tap a noodle crate to drop a portion into a free burner. The ring around
  the burner fills while they cook. When it turns green with a check, tap the burner (or
  drag the noodles onto a bowl) to drain them into a bowl. If you leave cooked noodles too
  long, the green ring drains and they overcook. Tap overcooked noodles to throw them out.
- **Broth, toppings and drinks**: tap a pot, a topping tray or a drink crate and it goes
  into the **selected bowl** (the one with the glowing mat and the little arrow). If that
  bowl can't take it (it already has a broth, that topping or a drink), it goes into the
  first bowl that can, and that bowl becomes the selected one. You can also drag an
  ingredient straight onto any bowl. A bowl holds at most 3 toppings.
- **Serve**: drag a bowl onto a customer, tap the customer, or tap a ready bowl twice.
  Tapping a customer always picks a bowl that exactly matches their order if there is
  one, so a stray tap never wastes a good bowl. An order bubble gets a green outline when
  the selected bowl matches it.
- **Mistakes**: drag a bowl to the bin (or select it and tap the bin) to start over.
- **Patience**: the bar at the bottom of each bubble drains from green to amber to red.
  Below 30% the bubble wobbles and the customer starts to sweat. VIP customers (gold
  crown, shades, gold tag on the bubble) wait less but pay 1.5x with bigger tips.
- **Pay**: the bowl's price (the sum of its ingredients) plus a tip that grows with the
  patience left, the tip jar and your reputation, plus a streak bonus of up to +4 for
  correct bowls in a row.
- **End of night**: the summary shows bowls served, customers lost, tips, best streak and
  the coins earned against the goal. 1 star at the goal, 2 stars at 1.4x, 3 stars at 1.85x.
  At 0 stars you keep the coins and replay the night, so you can always buy an upgrade and
  try again. There is no dead end.

## Controls

| Action | Touch / mouse | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Start noodles, ladle broth, add a topping or drink | tap the crate / pot / tray | focus it, Enter or Space |
| Drain cooked noodles | tap the burner, or drag it onto a bowl | focus it, Enter |
| Select a bowl | tap it | 1 to 4 |
| Serve | drag the bowl to the customer, tap the customer, or tap the ready bowl twice | focus the customer, Enter |
| Bin a bowl | drag it to the bin, or tap the bin | X, Delete or Backspace |
| Move the focus | – | arrow keys, Tab / Shift+Tab |
| Pause menu (Resume, Sound, Quit) | the pause button, top left | Esc or P |
| Sound on/off | the speaker button on the title, or in the pause menu | M |
| Summary / shop | tap the buttons and cards | Enter / 1 to 9, Esc for the title |

## Progression

| Night | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Shoyu broth, thin noodles, egg, nori, scallion. 2 customers at a time, 1 topping per order. A guided first bowl. |
| 2 | Chashu joins the menu. |
| 3 | Miso broth. 3 customers at a time, up to 2 toppings. |
| 4 | Wavy noodles. |
| 5 | Corn can be bought in the shop. |
| 6 | Naruto. 4 customers at a time, up to 3 toppings. |
| 7 | VIP customers start to appear. Spicy broth can be bought. |
| 8 | Tonkotsu broth. |
| 9 | Mushroom can be bought. |
| 10 | Udon. |
| 11 to 16 | Mostly 2 to 3 toppings. |
| 12 | Green tea: some orders add a drink. |
| 14 | Soda can be bought. |
| 17 to 20 | Mostly 3 toppings, the most VIPs and the shortest waits of the campaign. |
| 21+ | Endless nights: the goal rises by 5 coins a night and patience and arrival times keep shrinking down to fixed floors. |

The free menu items arrive on their night and are shown on the night's intro card. Shop
specials (corn, spicy broth, mushroom, soda) are optional: once bought, customers start
ordering them, and those orders pay more. Every night also gets a little longer
(90 s on night 1 to 120 s from night 16), customers arrive more often and wait less.

**Shop** (every night, prices in coins):

| Upgrade | Levels | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Counter space | 70, 170 | 2 → 4 bowls in prep |
| Extra burner | 60, 150 | 1 → 3 noodle burners |
| Hot stove | 40, 100, 180 | boil time 5 → 4 → 3.2 → 2.5 s |
| Lanterns | 50, 120, 220 | +12% patience per level |
| Tip jar | 60, 140 | +25% tips per level |
| Corn / Spicy broth / Mushroom / Soda | 90 / 140 / 120 / 130 | new, better-paid orders (from nights 5 / 7 / 9 / 14) |

**Reputation** (0 to 100, it starts at 50) rises by 1 or 2 with every served bowl and falls
by 5 (gave up) or 6 (wrong bowl). Higher reputation brings customers a little faster and
raises tips.

## Features

- Every ingredient has its own silhouette, so color is never the only cue: broths carry a
  glyph on the pot and on the order icon (a drop for shoyu, three soybeans for miso, a bone
  for tonkotsu, a chili for spicy), noodles are straight, zigzag or thick strands (also
  stamped on the crate), and the toppings are a half egg, an upright nori sheet, a pile of
  scallion rings, a chashu spiral, a scalloped naruto disc, a pile of square corn kernels
  and a shiitake cap with a cross cut. Tea is a wide cup, soda a pinched bottle.
- First-play tutorial on night 1: the clock and the patience bar stay frozen while an
  animated hand walks through the first bowl (start the noodles, ladle the broth, add the
  egg, wait for the ring, drain, drag the bowl to the customer). It follows the state, so
  it adapts to the order you do things in, and it points at the bin if you make a wrong
  bowl. A final card explains the patience bars and the bin, and the clock starts when it
  is dismissed.
- Juice: flying ingredients, broth splashes, pop on each addition, rising steam, bubbling
  pots, stars and floating coin text on a serve, a streak counter, happy hops and angry
  shakes, lanterns that sway gently. Nothing flashes.
- Sound is all WebAudio synthesis: noodle sizzle, broth pour, topping plop, quiet
  bubbling while noodles boil, a ready bell, drain whoosh, register chime and coin, the bin,
  an overcooked hiss, customer bells and grumbles, a closing-time jingle, and a soft lo-fi
  loop (76 bpm, swung hats, four 9th chords and a pentatonic pluck, quieter during service).
- Layout from `min(w, h)` units for any aspect ratio. Portrait: customers on top, the
  counter with the bowls and the bin in the middle, then rows of pots, crates and burners,
  and the topping trays. Landscape: a wide counter, and the kitchen in three groups (pots,
  crates over burners, trays). On short screens the order bubbles sit beside the
  customers. Every tap target is at least 44 px. The game re-lays itself out on resize.
- Text in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German, chosen with `getLanguage()`.
  Orders contain no text at all.

## Art direction: evening night market

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| dusk | `#3B2230` | sky, panels, page background |
| lantern | `#E8483B` | lanterns, awning, bowls, primary buttons |
| glow | `#FFC46B` | string lights, highlights, selection, titles |
| broth | `#E9B872` | miso broth, warm food tones |
| wood | `#A8683C` | counter, crates, chopsticks |
| nori | `#2E5E4E` | nori, tea crate, accents |
| cream | `#FFF3E0` | text on dark, bubbles, porcelain |
| ink | `#2A1620` | outlines and text on light (never pure black) |

All of these live in the `RR.C` palette object in `art.js`, and the food colors in `RR.F`.
The look is chunky cel shading: flat fills, a hard shade shape on one side, thick ink
outlines and solid offset shadows. The scene is a stall seen from behind the counter: a
striped awning with hanging paper lanterns, two rows of string lights, the silhouettes of
other stalls, a wooden counter and a dark kitchen. Customers are round, stylized
characters (round, cat, bear, fox, rabbit and raccoon folk) with hoodies, scarves, beanies,
caps, headphones or glasses. Cream text on dusk and ink text on cream both pass WCAG AA.

## Files

`index.html` (the SDK script first), `ytsdk.js` (the shared wrapper, unmodified),
`shift.js` (content tables, day configuration, order generator, pricing and the whole shift
simulation; it has no DOM code, so it also runs in Node), `i18n.js` (strings), `art.js`
(palette, food, stations, customers, backdrop), `audio.js` (synthesized sound and the
loop), `game.js` (layout, input, rendering, tutorial, summary, shop, lifecycle).

## Playables compliance

- **SDK calls** all go through `PlayablesSDK`:
  - `init({storageKey:'ramen-rush_save'})` runs first.
  - The title frame is drawn synchronously and then `firstFrameReady()` is called.
  - `load()` and `getLanguage()` run next, then `gameReady()` is called once the title
    accepts input (about 100 to 200 ms in the smoke test).
  - `onPause` stops the rAF loop (the shift clock, patience and burners freeze) and
    suspends the AudioContext. `onResume` restarts them and, during a shift, shows a
    "Tap to continue" overlay so play never resumes by surprise. The in-game pause menu
    also freezes the shift.
  - `onAudioChange` and `isAudioEnabled()` are ANDed with the in-game sound toggle. The
    AudioContext is created on the first gesture, and the loop is scheduled from the game
    loop, so it stops when the game is paused or muted.
  - `sendScore(total)` reports the **total coins earned over the whole career** (spending
    does not lower it) at the end of every night. It only ever grows, so it rewards both
    skill and long play.
  - `save()` is called at meaningful moments only: at the end of each night, after each
    purchase, when a night starts from the shop, when the tutorial is completed, on a new
    stall, and on the sound toggle.
- **Save format** (JSON, about 150 characters, far below 10 KiB):
  `{v:1, day: the next night to play, coins: wallet, total: lifetime coins (the score),
  best: best night cleared, rep, up: [counter, burner, stove, lanterns, tip jar] levels,
  sp: specials bitmask, st: best stars per night as a digit string (up to 200 nights),
  snd, tut, sh: 1 while waiting in the shop, played}`. Every field is validated and clamped
  on load. A finished night always resumes in the shop.
- **Size**: 7 shipped files, about 150 KiB in total. There are no assets, no external
  requests, no eval, Workers or WASM, and no text inputs. The game never asks for a name.
- **Checks**:
  - `node tools/check-bundle.mjs ramen-rush` gives PASS with no warnings.
  - `node tools/smoke.cjs ramen-rush <dir>` gives SMOKE PASS.
  - `node tools/ramen-rush-verify.cjs` gives VERIFY PASS. It checks makeability: for
    nights 1 to 40 and every set of specials a player could own by then (including none,
    the minimal upgrade path), about 240,000 generated orders are checked. Every
    ingredient must be on the menu with a station, toppings must be distinct and fit in a
    bowl, and every order is actually built and served in a cheapest-equipment shift.
    Every menu item must also show up in orders, and one player on the cheapest burner
    must be able to make any single order within 60% of its patience. It also checks
    goals: a simulated player taps at human speed (casual 1.25 s, reasonable 0.95 s, good
    0.65 s per tap, with a few wrong toppings), only reacts to what is on screen, and
    plays the real `shift.js` simulation. The reasonable player on a sensible upgrade path
    must clear every campaign night on the first try at least 85% of the time and reach
    night 30. A good player must earn 3 stars, a casual player must get past night 10, and
    a reasonable player who never upgrades must get past night 6. `--verbose` prints the
    per-night table.
  - A Playwright playthrough (portrait 390×844 with touch and landscape 1280×720 and
    844×390 with the mouse) plays the tutorial and full nights with real taps and drags,
    goes through the summary and the shop, buys upgrades, opens the pause menu, checks the
    "Tap to continue" overlay after a system pause, and drives the game with the keyboard
    only.
- `window.__ramenDebug.info()` is a small read-only test hook: scene, save, layout rects
  and the shift state (orders, bowls, burners), which the playthrough uses to choose which
  real taps to make. It sends nothing anywhere and does nothing unless it is called.
